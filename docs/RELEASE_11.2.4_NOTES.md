# TUFF 11.2.4

## The Top-K value lines up with the other sampling values

- In Settings > Generation > Sampling, the K value was drawn right after its title, at the left of the row, while its stepper sat at the far right. It now sits beside the stepper at the right edge, where the Temperature and P rows show their values and where the Inspector already showed it.
- Nothing else on the page moves: at 1,080 points wide the only pixels that differ from 11.2.3 are in that row.

## Compatibility

This patch uses the existing inference engines. Models, chats and settings are preserved. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized.
